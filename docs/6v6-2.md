## 2.1. Classlimits

Class counts are limited as follows:

- 2 Scouts
- 2 Soldiers
- 1 Pyro
- 1 Demoman
- 1 Heavy
- 1 Engineer
- 1 Medic
- 1 Sniper
- 2 Spies

Players switching to Spectator and/or from Spectator to change classes more quickly is strictly forbidden.

---

## 2.2. Maps

In the 6v6 format, 5CP maps (such as Process) and KOTH maps (such as Product) are played.

### 5CP

The time limit is set to 30 minutes (mp_timelimit 30) and the win limit to 5 rounds (mp_winlimit 5). The round timer is set to 4 minutes. The first team to reach five points, or the team with the highest amount of points once the map timer runs out, wins the map. During the group stage, if the map score is tied once the time limit is reached both teams will draw. During playoffs teams are supposed to run infinite overtime. See section below.

#### Infinite overtime during playoffs

The Progressive Ruleset incorporates [this plugin](https://github.com/ozfortress/Enhanced-Match-Timer/) that creates a dynamic win condition. The 5CP round win limit gets reduced to the current highest score +1 when the match timer runs out. For example, if the score is 1-2 when the map timer runs out, the win limit will be set to 3 and round continues.

The specifics of the progressive ruleset playoffs will look like this:

- The map timer for 5CP maps will be 25 minutes.
- The round timer for 5CP maps will be 4 minutes.
- If a team scores a total of 5 rounds before the end of the map timer, they win the map (winlimit 5).
- If neither team has scored a total of 5 rounds once the map timer runs out, the round winlimit is set to the current highest round score +1. The match continues until one team reaches the new round winlimit.

You can activate this ruleset by exec’ing `etf2l_6v6_5cp_prog.cfg`.

### KOTH (King Of The Hill)

There is no time limit. A win limit of 3 rounds (mp_winlimit 3) is set. The first team to win three rounds in the map wins.

## 2.3. Rosters must contain a minimum of six players

A team’s roster must consist of a minimum of six players to be eligible to sign up for 6v6 competitions.

---

## 2.4. Minimum of five players needed to play a match

Teams must field at least five players to play a scheduled 6v6 match. A team can ask their opponent to allow a merc to play for their team, following the rules of the [merc clause](/docs/6v6-1#13-mercenary-clause). If you are not able to field six players, you can choose to play 5v6.

A team that is not able to field five players will receive a Default Loss
